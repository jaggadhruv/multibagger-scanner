Method

Every check is a fixed yes or no. A check with missing data counts as not met. Thresholds live in config.py.

Gate 1 · Proven business

  • 1. Revenue growth of at least 15% a year over 3 years
  • 2. Revenue higher in each of the last 3 years
  • 3. Return on invested capital of at least 15%, averaged over 3 years
  • 4. Free cash flow positive in at least 3 of the last 4 years
  • 5. Free cash flow of at least 60% of net profit over 3 years
  • 6. Financial debt (leases excluded) of no more than 3 times the latest operating profit
  • 7. Share count growing no more than 4% a year over 3 years

Gate 2 · Early / inflecting

  • 8. Operating margin at least 1 percentage point(s) above 3 years ago, and not down more than 1.5 on last year
  • 9. Latest-year growth of at least 12% and at least 60% of the 3-year pace; latest quarter up at least 8% on a year ago when that figure is available

Gate 3 · Chart strength

  • 10. Weekly Supertrend (ATR 10, multiplier 2.5) on BUY
  • 11. Emerging (BUY for 12 weeks or less): price above the 200-day average. Established: price above a rising 200-day average and within 15% of the 52-week high
  • 12. Emerging: ahead of the S&P 500 over 3 months. Established: ahead over both 6 and 12 months

Not investment advice. This is a screening tool for research. The checklist has not been validated against past returns; the track record above is the only evidence it will ever produce, and it needs months to mean anything. Company numbers come from free data sources and can be wrong or out of date, so confirm them in the company's own filings before acting. Management quality, governance and fraud risk are not measured here.
